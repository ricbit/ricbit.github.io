Holy Water — discrete particle simulation

A full bottle starts holy. Tap-water particles enter as a coherent flow front and push particles out. Local contact can convert particles according to a strict “more than 50% holy neighbors” rule.

Touching means the 4 particles sharing an edge (up, down, left, right). Each tap particle has a continuous charge from 0% to 100%. While it touches a strict majority of fully holy particles, its charge rises continuously; otherwise it drains continuously. At 100% it becomes holy. Gray → blue shows the current charge.
